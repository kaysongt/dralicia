# Official appearance-identification artwork

Retrieved November 2, 2026 at the site owner's request to show the BET and
Nick Cannon @ Night marks in the homepage hero.

## bet-logo.svg

Original, unchanged BET white square mark (320 × 320).
Official source: https://www.bet.com/svg/BET_Logo_Hero_Square_Large_100K_CMYK_White.svg
Discovered in the official BET site's page markup.

## nick-cannon-at-night-logo.svg

An SVG viewBox displays the complete cyan/pink show title from BET's official
series key art. It embeds the original, unchanged 1600 × 900 JPEG; the viewBox
is `55 155 910 355`. No lettering, colors, or image pixels are redrawn. The view
excludes the series premiere date and host portrait so it does not confuse the
October 20 series premiere with Dr. Alicia's October 27 guest appearance.

Official key-art source:
https://public-assets-pressexpress.s3.amazonaws.com/assets/pages/images/2026/10/13/image_7877545131791911315147-foibpwfk.png
(The original server filename is .png, but the file's MIME/bytes are JPEG.)

Official BET announcement containing that image:
https://www.paramountpressexpress.com/bet/shows/nick-cannon-night/releases/?view=113396-bet-launches-nick-cannon-night-bringing-candid-conversation-to-late-night-premiering-tuesday-october-20

The original podcast-era mark from the show's own website includes WONDERY.
It is deliberately not used here: this feature identifies the BET television
appearance, using the corresponding BET-era series artwork instead.

These marks are not represented as public domain or unrestricted. Use is limited
to factual identification of the verified appearance; no sponsorship,
partnership, or endorsement of Dr. Alicia's services is asserted.
